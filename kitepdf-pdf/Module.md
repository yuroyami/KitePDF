# Module kitepdf-pdf

The PDF engine.

- `PdfDocument` and `PdfPage` read and render PDF files: text, search, metadata, outlines, annotations, form fields, encryption and signatures.
- `PdfBuilder` creates PDF files.
- `PdfEditor` fills forms, stamps pages, changes pages, redacts content and saves the result.
- `PdfSigner` prepares a signature field and embeds the signature that your app makes.

See [Reading PDFs](https://yuroyami.github.io/KitePDF/reading/), [Creating PDFs](https://yuroyami.github.io/KitePDF/writing/) and [Editing and redaction](https://yuroyami.github.io/KitePDF/editing/).
