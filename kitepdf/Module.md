# Module kitepdf

The umbrella artifact. It brings the readers of every format (PDF, EPUB, CBZ, SVG, and XPS or OpenXPS) in one dependency.

It adds `KiteDoc`, which opens a document when you do not know its format. When you need one format only, add its artifact, such as `kitepdf-pdf` or `kitepdf-xps`, and call its own `open` function. See [Loading a document](https://yuroyami.github.io/KitePDF/loading/).
